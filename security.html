Important Note

This page is a general summary, not a complete technical specification.

Tomb of Light uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to reduce risk, protect information, and support secure operation of the public website and platform-facing workflows. Specific controls, configurations, and vendors may change over time and are not fully described on this page.

Implemented Security Controls

Current safeguards protect accounts, customer workspaces, payments, uploads, and administrator actions.

The controls below describe the implemented Tomb of Light application posture as of August 21, 2026.

Account and Sign-In Protection

Passwords are one-way hashed with bcrypt. New passwords require at least 12 characters. Sign-in and verification endpoints use rate limits and account lockouts. Customers, officers, and administrators can opt into authenticator-based multi-factor authentication; when enabled, it is enforced at sign-in.

Time-Limited, Revocable Sessions

Authenticated sessions are signed and time-limited. Logout, password changes, and security resets revoke prior sessions server-side. Browser bearer credentials and account context are kept within the active browser tab instead of persistent local storage.

Server-Enforced Access Boundaries

Role and permission checks are enforced by the API, not only by hidden interface controls. Customer access is scoped by account, project, active entitlement, workspace membership, and assigned capabilities. Officer dashboards are reduced to their authorized operational domain.

Governed Administrator Execution

Live Control Center writes run through the Continuity Kernel with a named action, reason, confirmation, idempotency protection, before-and-after evidence, audit events, and an emergency execution kill switch. Customer preview remains read-only; edits use explicit governed operations.

Upload Controls and Quarantine

Uploads are restricted by supported file types and size limits. Malware scanning is fail-closed by default: infected files, scanner errors, or unavailable scanning are quarantined and blocked from ordinary download rather than treated as safe.

Payment Data Separation

Card entry occurs on Stripe-hosted payment surfaces. Tomb of Light does not accept or store raw card numbers or CVC values. Payment events use Stripe signature verification and idempotent processing, while administrative Stripe writes are governed and audit logged.

Transport and Browser Protections

Tomb of Light uses HTTPS for information in transit. The API applies transport, anti-sniffing, anti-framing, referrer, permissions, and no-store headers. Website pages apply a Content Security Policy that limits executable scripts and network connections to approved sources.

Private-by-Default Legacy Content

Private workspace materials are not published or placed on-chain by default. Public or collectible workflows use separate readiness and approval gates intended to limit output to approved public-safe material and metadata.

Private Family Records

Private family content is intended for controlled systems, not casual public exposure.

Tomb of Light is designed around the principle that private family history, portraits, lineage records, and related materials deserve careful handling. Private family content is not intended for public display by default and may be handled through protected workflows, access controls, and account-based experiences where applicable.

Account Protection

Access depends on user behavior as well as platform controls.

01

Password Discipline

Users should choose a strong, unique password and keep credentials confidential.

02

Access Limitation

Administrative and operational access should be limited to people with a legitimate business need.

03

Workflow Review

Tomb of Light may review suspicious activity, misuse reports, and workflow anomalies to protect users and platform integrity.

Uploads and Sensitive Materials

Users should submit only what is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

Verification and family-record workflows may involve sensitive materials. Tomb of Light asks users not to upload Social Security cards, full Social Security numbers, payment card information, or other unnecessary sensitive information unless specifically requested for a defined workflow.

Responsible Disclosure

Report a potential security issue responsibly and directly.

If you believe you have identified a security issue affecting the public website or a Tomb of Light workflow, report it responsibly and do not access, alter, expose, or misuse information or systems you are not authorized to use.

What to Include

  • A clear description of the issue
  • The page, feature, or workflow affected
  • Steps to reproduce, if known
  • Relevant screenshots or technical context

Expected Conduct

  • Do not access data you are not authorized to view
  • Do not disrupt services or availability
  • Do not perform destructive testing
  • Do not expose private information publicly

How to Contact

Send security-related reports to support@tomboflight.com with the subject line “Security Report.”

Limits

No system can promise absolute security.

Security is an ongoing discipline, not a one-time claim. No website, platform, storage system, transmission method, or account system can be guaranteed to remain fully protected at all times.

Tomb of Light does not claim SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS certification, end-to-end encryption, or another independent security certification unless a current written statement expressly confirms it. Card processing is delegated to Stripe-hosted systems, but that does not make the entire Tomb of Light platform PCI certified.
